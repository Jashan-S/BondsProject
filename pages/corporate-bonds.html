What this page is, and is not

These are market-wide statistics published by FINRA from TRACE, its record of reported corporate bond trades. FINRA owns the data. It is shown here free of charge for personal, non-commercial use, it is not part of any paid Basispoint plan, and it may not be copied or redistributed from this site.

This page does not show individual bonds or prices. Screening single issues or checking a quote against recent trades needs licensed trade-level data, which Basispoint does not have yet.

If you are new to corporates, the idea to take from this page is liquidity: a small number of large bonds trade constantly while tens of thousands of issues barely trade at all. The credit spread you are paid always includes compensation for how hard a bond is to sell.
