Policy rates against the market

The 2-year yield is roughly the market's average expected policy rate over the next two years. When it sits well above the central bank's rate, markets expect rates to go up on average; well below, they expect cuts.

Curve shape

Each slope is the longer yield minus the shorter one. A falling slope is a flattening curve; below zero the curve is inverted.

What drove the US 10-year

The 10-year yield is roughly the 10-year real yield (from inflation-protected Treasuries) plus breakeven inflation, the inflation rate the market is pricing in. Splitting a move between them shows whether investors are demanding more real return or expecting more inflation.
